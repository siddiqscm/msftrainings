## Voice & Messaging

### Brand Voice
- **Professional:** Enterprise-focused, expert-level
- **Practical:** Real-world scenarios, hands-on approach
- **Trusted:** Certified trainers, proven success rates
- **Clarity:** Clear, jargon-explained, action-oriented

### Key Messages
1. **Certification Excellence:** 95% first-time exam success rate
2. **Hands-on Learning:** Live labs, real-world scenarios, practical implementation
3. **Expert Instructors:** Certified trainers with enterprise experience
4. **Corporate Ready:** Customizable training for enterprise needs
5. **Proven Track Record:** 500+ professionals trained

### Tone Guidelines
- Use active voice ("Master Business Central" vs "Business Central can be mastered")
- Address audience directly ("You'll learn X" vs "One learns X")
- Emphasize outcomes ("Pass certification confidently" vs "Get trained")
- Use inclusive language ("Your certification journey" vs "Our program")

## Accessibility Standards

### Color Contrast
- **Text on Blue:** White (#FFF) for ≥4.5:1 ratio
- **Text on Light:** Dark Blue (#003DA5) for ≥4.5:1 ratio
- **Interactive Elements:** Cyan accents on dark for ≥3:1 ratio

### Font Sizing
- **Minimum:** 12px for body text
- **Line Height:** ≥1.4 for readability
- **Letter Spacing:** 0.5px for headings

### Mobile Responsiveness
- **Logo:** 32px on mobile, 40px on tablet, 48px on desktop
- **Text:** Scale proportionally: h1 36px (mobile) → 48px (desktop)
- **Buttons:** Minimum 44px height for touch targets
